Setup

This unit is an access point: every port is bridged into the LAN, it takes its address from the network's DHCP server and it runs no WAN, DHCP or DNS of its own. Its Wi-Fi and bridge come from the image's access-point settings, so there is no router setup wizard to run here.

Things you may want to look at on an access point:

  • Interfaces: the bridge, its ports and its address.
  • Monitoring: Wi-Fi clients joining and leaving (system log).
  • System: accounts, backups, firmware and services.

Setup wizard

Name the router, set its clock, pick the WAN and LAN ports, say how the WAN connects (DHCP, a static address or a PPPoE login) and whether IPv6 is used, give the LAN an address and (optionally) a DHCP pool. Everything is saved in the configuration, applied to the system now and again at every boot, and stays editable on the System, Interfaces, NTP and DHCP pages.

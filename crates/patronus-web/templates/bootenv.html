Boot environments

On a ZFS install every update first takes a snapshot of the system, and each boot environment keeps its own kernels. Pick the environment the router starts next time, roll back to a snapshot taken before an update, or free space by deleting old ones. Your configuration, logs and other data live outside the boot environments and are kept whichever one you boot. Back to System

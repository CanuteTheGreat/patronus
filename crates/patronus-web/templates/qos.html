Traffic shaping

One queue discipline per interface, applied with tc: CAKE at a bandwidth (fair queueing per flow and host, fixes bufferbloat), fq_codel (no limit) or a plain rate limit. Traffic leaving the interface is shaped: put it on the WAN to shape upload, on the LAN to shape download. Set the rate a little below the link speed so the queue builds here.

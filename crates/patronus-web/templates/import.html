Moving from pfSense, OPNsense or OpenWrt? Load the old router's configuration file and Patronus takes over its interfaces, VLANs, PPPoE, gateways and routes, aliases, firewall and NAT rules, DHCP and DNS, WireGuard, OpenVPN and IPsec. Preview first: nothing changes until you press Import. Anything that has no Patronus equivalent is listed, never silently dropped. Back to System
